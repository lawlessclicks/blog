# Why Most Law Firm Websites Fail Google's E-E-A-T Standards and How to Fix It

![E-E-A-T Standards](https://lawlessclicks.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/lawless_professional_editorial_photo_laptop_showing_declining_dc8bd267-6b42-4ed9-861c-c6a0ba5a1b1c_3-1-1.png)

*Originally published on [Lawless Clicks](https://lawlessclicks.com/ai-geo-optimization-for-lawyers/law-firm-eeat-guide/)*

Google does not treat every website equally. Since the introduction of E-E-A-T as a core component of its Search Quality Rater Guidelines, law firm websites that fail to demonstrate these signals are losing ground to competitors who get it right.

## What E-E-A-T Actually Means for Law Firms

**Experience** refers to first-hand involvement with the subject matter.

**Expertise** is about credentials and depth of knowledge.

**Authoritativeness** measures how the broader legal community recognizes your firm.

**Trustworthiness** is the foundation — the most important member of the E-E-A-T family.

## Where Most Law Firm Websites Fall Short

- No author attribution on YMYL content
- Thin practice area pages lacking depth
- Missing trust signals (address, reviews, SSL)
- No case results or social proof

## How AI Search Engines Use E-E-A-T Signals

ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews use similar quality signals to decide which sources to cite.

## The Competitive Advantage

E-E-A-T is not a buzzword. For YMYL sites like law firms, it is the price of admission.
